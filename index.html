Memdeklaro is a self-declaration of identity: A way to express your chosen name and personal ties, independent from centralized third parties.

Generate your self-declaration for free and use it in your community, web of trust or peer-to-peer network.

Build up your reputation, portfolio or persona on a universally accessible foundation, using a physical self-declaration card or digital credential.

Building a Fairer Future

Memdeklaro developed the self-declaration of identity as a universally accessible identity document, which does not require the permission of a nation-state, birth parents or other third parties.

This helps to build a free world where personal beliefs, character and effort are more important than where you were born or who you were born to.
